Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Roseify is built to collect as little as possible. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

1. No accounts, no tracking

  • You don't need an account to use Roseify.
  • Roseify doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

2. Data stored on your device

Your settings (the Riot IDs you save, your preferred server and selected filters) are stored only in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you search, and you can delete them anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Data from the Riot Games API

When you search for a player, Roseify asks the official Riot Games API for that player's public game data: Riot ID, level, rank, champion mastery, match history and live game information. This is the same public data shown in the League client and on other stats sites.

  • To keep the app fast and stay within Riot's request limits, Roseify temporarily caches API responses on its server. Finished matches may be cached longer, because they never change.
  • Builds, pick rates and win rates are calculated from a sample of public matches. These statistics are aggregated, and they don't identify individual players.
  • Searches aren't linked to you or stored as a history of who searched for whom.

4. Server logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, time and requested page) for security and reliability. These logs aren't used to identify or profile visitors.

5. Future features

Planned Valorant features will require players to sign in with their Riot account (Riot Sign On) and explicitly agree to share their data. This policy will be updated before any such feature launches.

6. Your choices

If you'd like cached data about your Riot account removed from Roseify's server, or have any privacy question, contact us at github.com/ryucagan/Roseify/issues. Riot's own privacy practices are described in the Riot Games Privacy Notice.

7. Children

Roseify isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
