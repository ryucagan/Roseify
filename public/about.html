About Roseify

Roseify is a free, fan-made stats app for League of Legends. It uses the official Riot Games API to show player profiles, match history, live games, and champion builds and tier lists for every game mode. Valorant support is planned.

What you can do

  • Look up any player by Riot ID to see their rank, champion mastery and match history, with full scoreboards. Roseify finds the player's server automatically.
  • Check a live game to see both teams' champions, runes, ranks and most-played champions.
  • Browse builds and tier lists for every mode and lane: full rune pages, item builds that match each rune page, skill order, counters and best teammates.

Where the data comes from

All player and match data comes from the official Riot Games API. Builds, pick rates and win rates are calculated by Roseify from a sample of recent public matches, so they're estimates, and every number shows how many games it's based on. Champion, item and rune images come from Riot's Data Dragon and the community-run CommunityDragon.

Who makes it
